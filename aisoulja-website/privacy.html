Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by AISoulja, a creator who sells digital products such as e-books, posters and banners. You can reach us at Levrosky@gmail.com.

What this website collects

This website itself does not ask for your personal details, does not use tracking or advertising cookies, and does not run analytics. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may see your IP address when the page loads.

Purchases

All purchases are handled by SuperProfile, our payment and delivery partner. When you buy, you enter your details (such as your name, email address, phone number and payment information) on SuperProfile’s checkout page, not on this website. SuperProfile uses them to process your payment and send you your product, under its own privacy policy. We receive the order details needed to deliver your product and support you, such as your name, email and what you bought. We never see or store your full card or bank details.

How we use your information

We use your order details only to deliver your product, answer your questions, and resolve problems with your order. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we keep your message and email address so we can reply and help you.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing Levrosky@gmail.com. We will reply within a reasonable time.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
