Refund Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

All sales are final

Our products, such as e-books, posters and banners, are digital files that are delivered instantly after payment. Because they cannot be “returned”, all sales are final and we do not offer refunds or exchanges.

Before you buy

Please read the product description carefully before purchasing. If you have a question about whether a product is right for you, email us first at Levrosky@gmail.com.

If something goes wrong

We will always help if:

  • you paid but did not receive your download link (please check your spam folder first),
  • your file is damaged or will not open, or
  • you were charged twice for the same order.

In these cases, email Levrosky@gmail.com with your name, the email you used at checkout, and your payment details. We will send you a working file or, for a duplicate charge, refund the extra payment.
